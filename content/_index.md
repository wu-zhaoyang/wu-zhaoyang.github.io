<h1 class="profile-name">Zhaoyang Wu <span class="profile-name-zh">伍昭阳</span></h1>
<p class="profile-affiliation">
<img class="profile-school-logo" src="/images/swust-logo.png" alt="SWUST" />
UG Student @ Southwest University of Science and Technology
</p>
<p class="profile-bio-en">Zhaoyang Wu is an undergraduate student majoring in Electrical Engineering and Automation at Southwest University of Science and Technology. Since joining the <a href="https://info.swust.edu.cn/tssys/main.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Robot Technology Used for Special Environments Key Laboratory of Sichuan Province</a> and the Intelligent Systems and Smart Services Innovation Practice Class, he has been exploring interdisciplinary research in power systems, artificial intelligence, and multimodal perception. His current interests include AI-enabled power system fault detection and localization, optimal PMU placement, spatio-temporal graph neural networks, and robust inertial gait recognition. In the future, he hopes to further advance research in intelligent power systems, graph-based temporal modeling, and multimodal perception for intelligent energy systems and embodied robotic applications.</p>
<p class="profile-bio-zh">伍昭阳，本科，电气工程及其自动化专业。自大二加入特殊环境机器人技术四川省重点实验室及“智能系统与智慧服务”创新实践班以来，他开始在电力系统人工智能领域进行学习和探索，专注于故障检测与定位。此后，他继续参与最优 PMU 配置、时空图神经网络、伪装惯性步态识别等研究，参与算法实现、实验分析、数据集构建和论文写作，并在智能电力系统、图学习、时序建模和鲁棒感知上展现出浓厚兴趣。未来，他希望在人工智能驱动的电力系统分析、复杂场景下的鲁棒故障诊断，以及多模态感知赋能的智能能源系统与具身机器人应用领域继续探索。</p>
